# Data Quality SaaS
## Business Scenario
DataTrust is a B2B SaaS company that helps organizations monitor,
measure, and improve the quality and reliability of their analytical data.

## Business Requirement

The DataTrust team needs a reliable analytics platform to monitor
data quality across customers, data sources, pipelines, and data assets.

The platform should help the business identify data-quality problems,
understand their impact, and track quality trends over time.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which customers have the most data-quality issues?
2. Which data assets have the highest number of quality failures?
3. Which data-quality dimensions fail most often?
4. Which pipelines have the highest failure rates?
5. Which data assets are stale or not refreshed on time?
6. How is data quality changing over time?
7. Which customers or assets require attention?
8. What percentage of monitored assets currently meet quality standards?
